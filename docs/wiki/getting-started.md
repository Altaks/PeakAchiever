PeakAchiever lets you pin the badges you want to chase, then shows during the run which ones you can still earn, how far along you are, and what you need.

## Install

1. Install [BepInExPack for PEAK](https://thunderstore.io/c/peak/p/BepInEx/BepInExPack_PEAK/), or let your mod manager do it.
2. Install PeakAchiever with a mod manager (Thunderstore Mod Manager, r2modman, Gale) and launch the game from it.
   By hand: copy `Altaks.PeakAchiever.dll` into `BepInEx/plugins/`.
3. Other players do not need the mod. Those who have it share their earned badges and see the host's team pins.

Tested with PEAK 2.6.b. The mod speaks the game's 15 languages and follows the language set in the game.

## Pin a badge

1. Open the pause menu, then the **Badges** page. It works in the airport too, so you can pick your targets before take-off.
2. Click a badge: a yellow pin marks it. Click it again to unpin it. The badge's tooltip says which a click will do.
3. Up to 5 badges can be pinned at once (1 to 12 in **Settings, General**).

The badges page helps you choose:

- **Stars** mark as many suggested badges as you have free pin slots: all doable on today's map, and compatible with your pins and with each other.
- A **red cross** marks a badge today's map cannot hold; its tooltip names the missing biome.
- A **faded** badge cannot share a run with one of your pins (no map has both biomes); its tooltip names the pin in the way.

## During the run

Your pinned badges show as cards in the top-right corner. Press **F6** to hide or show them (change the key in **Settings, Controls**). The cards hide while the pause menu is open.

The **Reading a card** page explains what each card shows.
