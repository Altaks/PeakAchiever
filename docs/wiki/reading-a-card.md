Each pinned badge is a dark, stitched card: the badge's icon, its name, the condition as the game words it, then its status.

## Status

| You see | It means |
| --- | --- |
| A yellow bar and `x / y` | A counter, this run. With an **All runs** chip, the game counts it over every run (meals cooked, height climbed...). |
| "Doable" | Nothing in the game can rule it out this run. |
| "Mesa, next biome" / "Mesa, in 2 biomes" | The badge can only be earned in that biome, and that is how far it is on this run's map. |
| "Now, in the Mesa" (yellow) | You are in the badge's biome: the moment to go for it. |
| "Holding so far" | A clean-run condition is still intact; the game checks it at the summit. |
| A heat, cold or spores bar, `max 4% / 10%` | The highest rate reached in the biome, against the limit. Near it the outline blinks and the margin left shows in orange. |
| A green check, "Earned" | Earned this run. After 5 seconds the card folds to one line, under the cards in play. |
| A red cross and a reason | It can no longer be earned this run. The card tears in two, falls to the end and stays there as one torn line. |
| "For an ally" chip, "You have it" | You pinned a badge you already have, to help a friend earn it. |

A red cross only ever appears when the game itself proves the badge is out of reach. When in doubt, the card stays "Doable".

## What a badge needs

Across the bottom of the card: the items the badge needs, as the game's own icons, with a count when several are needed.

- **Needs**: every item shown. **One of**: any one of them.
- **Helps**: items that make it easier, without being needed.

## Locators

Bellringer, Megaentomology and 24 Karat show an arrow under their icon, turned towards their target, with the distance:

- **Bellringer**: the nearest unlit belltower. The bar counts the belltowers the whole team has lit, against 5.
- **Megaentomology**: the nearest antlion.
- **24 Karat**: the Mesa tomb. Only the Ancient Idol counts, thrown into the Kiln.

A marker also shows over the target on screen. Turn it off in **Settings, General, PeakAchiever: on-screen markers**.

## Speed Climber

The run time on its bar, the ETA beside it (green within the hour, orange past it), then each biome's time, one a line: its time and its gap to the median of your past runs at the same ascent (red slower, green faster). The ETA shows once every biome left has been finished at least once with the mod.

## Item checklists

Foraging, Mycology, Advanced Mycology and Gourmand show every item that counts: dimmed until eaten, then in colour with a tick. Items this map does not seem to yield move to a fainter row, "Not seen on this map": a hint only.
