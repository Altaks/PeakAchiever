## The tracker does not show

- Pin at least one badge on the pause menu's **Badges** page.
- Press **F6** (or your key): the tracker may be hidden.
- The tracker hides while the pause menu is open.

## A card says "Doable" for a badge I cannot get

The mod only puts a red cross when the game itself proves the badge is out of reach this run. Some badges depend on things the game does not tell in advance (where a scout cannon or an antlion spawns, what luggage holds), so they stay "Doable".

## The ETA does not show

It needs every biome left to have been finished at least once with the mod installed, at the same ascent. Keep climbing: it appears once your history covers the rest of the map.

## After a game update

Errors show in `BepInEx/LogOutput.log`. Look for lines from `PeakAchiever` and report them on the mod's page with the game version.

## Two copies of the mod

If you installed the mod both by hand and with a mod manager, BepInEx loads only one of them. Remove one copy from `BepInEx/plugins/`.
